Pacsafe Redesigned

Emails are often glanced at for only about 2 seconds.

Reflecting Litmus insights, the message is therefore designed to be highly skimmable and immediately engaging.

After signing up through a discount pop-up, the first message promptly delivers on its promise,

so subscribers immediately receive the incentive and clearly understand expectations for future updates.

It also positions Pacsafe as a mission-led brand and builds trust through structured comparisons, customer testimonials, and a guided flow that encourages exploration rather than aggressive selling.
